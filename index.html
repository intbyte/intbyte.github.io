About

Hello, I’m Arnab Raha, a civil engineering student at the Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET). My interests lie in modeling, simulation, optimization, and the growing role of artificial intelligence in solving civil engineering problems. I’m particularly drawn to data-driven approaches that connect theoretical ideas with practical systems.

I spent a good part of my school years participating in programming contests, math olympiads, and working on platforms like Codeforces—often without notable achievements. But I kept going, mostly because I enjoyed thinking through problems, even when the results didn’t follow.

Over time, that persistence evolved into a more focused interest in computational methods. Alongside my academic work, I’ve developed experience with tools like QGIS, AutoCAD, and MATLAB, and programming in C, C++, C#, and Python. Earlier, I had strong interest in game development and learned Unity, and that passion had me the opportunity to secure 3rd place in GameJam of BUET CSE Fest 2024. I’ve also worked on projects ranging from CAD competitions to research-based studies and actively involved in clubs such as the American Concrete Institute, American Society of Civil Engineers, and the BUET Robotics Society. Outside of academics, I enjoy traveling and exploring new places, especially experiences that involve a sense of challenge or adventure

A key area of my work has been the modeling and optimization of metro rail headways in Dhaka using simulation and genetic algorithms. What began as a conceptual idea evolved into a more rigorous study, ultimately leading to a conference publication. More recently, I completed a project focused on analyzing particle dynamics from video for flocculation studies, which provided valuable experience in experimental analysis and data interpretation

I tend to think about things longer than I probably should—sometimes about problems that don’t really matter. But every now and then, those “unnecessary” thoughts end up leading somewhere interesting.

Projects

Video-Based Analysis of Floc Settling Dynamics Using Image Processing and Data-Driven Scaling with MATLAB
This project investigates the settling behavior of alum-precipitated kaolin flocs in a quiescent water column using a MATLAB-based image processing approach. By correlating floc morphological diameter with terminal settling velocity, the study evaluates deviations from Stokes’ Law and estimates the fractal dimension of the aggregates. The results highlight the influence of porous, irregular floc structures on settling dynamics and provide computational insights relevant to sedimentation process design in water treatment engineering.
